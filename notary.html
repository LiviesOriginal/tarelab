Before Your Appointment

What to bring.

Valid Photo ID

  • Driver's license
  • Passport
  • State-issued ID
  • Military ID

Unsigned Documents

Do not sign the document before the appointment. Signatures must happen in front of the notary.

Additional Signers

If others need to sign (co-borrowers, witnesses, etc.), they must be present with their own valid ID.
