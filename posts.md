# Welcome to the blog

Aug 22, 2026

This is your first post. To publish a new one, open **this file**, add your entry at the **top**, and re-upload it — the page updates itself, and you never touch the layout.

Each post is just three simple parts:

1. A line starting with `# ` — that's the **title**
2. The next line — the **date** (write it however you like)
3. Then your writing

Separate one post from the next with a line of three dashes on its own.

You can use *italics*, **bold**, `inline code`, and [links](https://example.com). Bullet lists work too:

- like this
- and this

> And blockquotes, if you want to pull something out.
