Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where is SPARQ available?+

We're launching Florida-first, where EV ownership is high and public charging is stretched thin. New regions are rolling out — add your address in the app to see what's live near you.

How does pricing work?+

Hosts set their own price. Depending on the listing, you'll pay either per kilowatt-hour of energy delivered or a flat rate for the session. Either way, you see the estimated total before you confirm the booking.

How do hosts get paid?+

Payments run through the app and payouts are sent to hosts automatically after each completed session. There's nothing to invoice and no cash to handle.

What do I need to become a host?+

A home EV charger you own or have permission to share, and the right to offer it (check any HOA, condo, or landlord rules first). List your connector type and speed, set your hours, and you're ready to accept requests.

Is it safe to let a stranger use my charger?+

Bookings are reviewed by the host, access details are shared only after you approve, and two-way reviews build accountability on both sides. You stay in control of when your charger is open and who you accept.

Which vehicles are supported?+

Any EV — just filter by your connector (Type 2, J1772, CCS, or NACS) to see chargers that fit. Compatibility is shown clearly on every listing.
