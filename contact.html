FAQ

Frequently asked.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope. Every project gets a fixed-fee proposal after the discovery call — no surprise invoices, no mystery hourly billing.

How long does it take?

Small projects (resume, single SOP) — one to two weeks. Larger engagements (full systems buildout, rebrand) — four to eight weeks. Timelines are locked in the proposal.

Do you work remotely?

Yes — most work is done remotely with regular video check-ins. In-person is available for local clients and notary services.

What if it's not a fit?

Then we don't move forward, and you get honest feedback on where to look next. The discovery call is free for a reason.
