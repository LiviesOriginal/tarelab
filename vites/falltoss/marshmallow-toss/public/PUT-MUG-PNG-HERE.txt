Drop your transparent mug art in this folder as:

    mug.png

That's it — with `npm run dev` running, the game picks it up automatically and
replaces the dashed "mug.png" placeholder. No rebuild needed.

Line the opening up with the (invisible) scoring ring using the three knobs in
src/game/constants.ts -> MUG_PLACEMENT:

    drawW      how wide to draw your image, in play units (the ring is ~156 wide)
    openXFrac  where the cup opening sits ACROSS your art  (0 = left … 1 = right)
    openYFrac  where the cup opening sits DOWN your art     (0 = top  … 1 = bottom)

The point (openXFrac, openYFrac) inside your image is pinned to the mug opening,
so dunks keep landing where the art shows. If dunks score above the drawn
opening, raise openYFrac; if they're off to a side, nudge openXFrac; if the
opening is a different size than the target, tweak MUG.rimRx / MUG.rimRy.

You can delete this .txt file once mug.png is in place.
