01 / The idea

Not round.
Not ordinary.

Between is a premium ice cream sandwich brand built around one unmistakable signature: the square.

Thoughtful layers. Beautiful contrast. Unexpected flavor combinations. Every sandwich is made to be held, photographed, shared, and remembered.

04 / Our story

The best things happen between.

Between is a celebration of the space where two good things become something better: cookie and cream, crisp and soft, familiar and unexpected.

Our sandwiches are intentionally square, generously layered, and designed with the kind of detail usually reserved for the last bite of a very good dessert.
